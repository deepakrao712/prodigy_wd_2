Smart Digital Watch with Stopwatch Features
This repository contains a Smart Digital Watch with built-in stopwatch features. The application includes a visually appealing watch face with a 3D bezel and glass reflection effect. It features functional stopwatch controls like Start, Pause, Lap, and Reset.

Additionally, the page offers sound feedback on button clicks and automatically adjusts the background based on the time of day (Day/Night theme). The app is fully responsive, and the design adapts beautifully across different screen sizes.

Features
Digital Watch Display: A real-time running digital watch face with a 3D bezel and glass reflection effect.

Stopwatch Controls: Functional Start, Pause, Lap, and Reset buttons to measure time accurately.

Lap Tracking: Record multiple laps and display the time of each lap.

Sound Feedback: Button clicks are accompanied by a realistic sound effect.

Day/Night Background Theme: The background adjusts based on the time of day. Light theme (day) is shown between 6 AM and 6 PM, while a dark theme (night) is displayed after 6 PM.

Responsive Design: The page is fully responsive, ensuring it works well on mobile, tablet, and desktop devices.
Orbitron Font: Used for the digital display, providing a futuristic look.
Button Sound Effect: A free button click sound from SoundJay.
